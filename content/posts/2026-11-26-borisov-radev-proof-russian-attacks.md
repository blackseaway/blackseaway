Boyko Borisov, leader of Bulgaria's GERB party, has criticised Prime Minister Rumen Radev for continuing to demand proof of Russian attacks on commercial ships in the Black Sea, even after Vladimir Putin himself publicly acknowledged that Russia is striking such vessels in response to Ukrainian actions.

Speaking in Montana, a city in north-western Bulgaria, Borisov said that when the Russian president openly admits to targeting merchant ships, Radev's ongoing scepticism is hard to justify.

Borisov warned that a failure to respond firmly could harm next year's tourist season and further drive up insurance and shipping costs, as the Black Sea is already classified as a high-risk zone.

## Background

Since October, several merchant ships have been hit by drones off the Bulgarian coast, and at least one has sunk. In mid-October Radev said that the logic of the attacks pointed to Russia and summoned the Russian ambassador, but that Sofia had no solid evidence to formally assign blame while the investigation continued.

Since 16 September the London insurance market has listed the whole Black Sea, except the territorial waters of Türkiye, Romania, Bulgaria and Georgia, as a war-risk area. Read more in our analysis: [The whole Black Sea is now a war-risk zone](whole-black-sea-war-risk-zone.html).
