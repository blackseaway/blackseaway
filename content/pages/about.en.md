**Black Sea Way** follows how the war at sea changes the way goods move across the Black Sea: attacks on ports and ships, drifting mines, war-risk insurance, freight costs, and what all of this means for trade in Romania, Bulgaria and the wider region.

## What we do

- **News monitoring.** Every hour our system collects news from international, Romanian and Bulgarian media, tags it by topic and place, and translates the headlines into English, Romanian and Bulgarian. Each headline links to the original publisher. We do not republish full texts.
- **Data.** We put the key figures in one place: insurance premiums, cargo volumes, incidents. Each figure carries its date and source. The charts and the map are calculated automatically from the news we collect.
- **Analysis.** Our editorial team writes explainers and investigations for logistics professionals, journalists and policymakers.

## Who we are

Black Sea Way is an independent initiative of a group of journalists. Editor-in-chief: **Roman Bobeshto**.

## Principles

We name our sources and link to them. We separate facts from opinion. We correct mistakes openly. Automatically collected headlines are the responsibility of their original publishers, and machine translations are marked as such.
