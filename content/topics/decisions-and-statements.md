The war at sea now reaches the coasts of two EU and NATO members, Romania and Bulgaria, and the waters of Türkiye, which controls access to the Black Sea. Each new attack brings statements, emergency meetings and decisions.

## Who decides what

- **Türkiye** mediates between Russia and Ukraine and applies the Montreux Convention, which limits warships of non-coastal states in the Black Sea.
- **Romania and Bulgaria** respond to attacks near their coasts, scramble aircraft, close sea areas for exercises and ask allies for help with surveillance.
- **Ukraine** proposes safe-passage arrangements and compensates owners of ships damaged by Russian strikes.
- **NATO and the EU** add surveillance capacity and fund the new maritime security hub.
