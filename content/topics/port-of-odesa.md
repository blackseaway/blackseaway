The three ports of Greater Odesa, Odesa, Chornomorsk and Pivdennyi, handle most of Ukraine's seaborne trade, above all grain, vegetable oil, iron ore and containers. Since August 2023 they have worked through the Ukrainian maritime corridor along the western Black Sea.

The ports are regularly targeted by Russian drones and missiles, which damage terminals, grain silos, power supply and ships at berth or on approach. Even so, cargo keeps moving, protected by air defence, mine clearance and state-backed war-risk insurance.
