Mines were the first maritime danger of the war in the Black Sea. In 2022 hundreds were laid off the Ukrainian coast, and many broke loose and drifted south towards Romania, Bulgaria and the Bosphorus.

## From mines to drones

Today the picture has changed. Unexploded aerial drones and sea drones now make up almost all explosive objects found at sea. They wash up on beaches, drift into shipping lanes and are sometimes caught in fishing nets.

## Who clears them

Romania, Bulgaria and Türkiye run a joint mine-countermeasures task group, MCM Black Sea, which has neutralised more than 150 mines. In late 2026 the three countries agreed to widen its mission to protecting critical infrastructure such as offshore gas installations, cables and pipelines. National navies also publish navigational warnings with the positions of found objects and recommended safe routes.

## Where to check current warnings

Before and during a voyage, check NAVAREA III warnings, Turkish NAVTEX and the Romanian coastal warnings. Links to all of them are on our [Data & map](../stats.html) page.
