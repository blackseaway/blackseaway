Every ship entering or leaving the Black Sea passes through the Bosphorus and the Dardanelles. This gives Türkiye a unique role: it controls passage under the Montreux Convention, runs the Turkish Straits vessel traffic service and acts as the main mediator between Russia and Ukraine on shipping.

The war at sea now reaches Turkish waters and Turkish-owned ships, which has pushed Ankara to propose a new safe-passage arrangement for civilian shipping.
