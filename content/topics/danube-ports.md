The lower Danube has become one of Ukraine's most important trade routes since 2022. Grain and other cargo from Izmail, Reni and smaller river ports is shipped down the river and reaches the Black Sea through Romania, mostly via the Sulina canal.

The route is under constant pressure. Russian drones target port facilities on the Ukrainian bank, sometimes only a few hundred metres from Romanian territory, and ships have been hit near the Danube mouth. Reinsurers have been restricting cover for the Black Sea and the Danube, which raises the cost of every voyage.
