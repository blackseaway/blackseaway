Novorossiysk is Russia's largest port on the Black Sea and one of its main outlets for oil and grain exports. Together with Tuapse, the ports of occupied Crimea and the Kerch Strait, it is at the centre of Ukraine's campaign against Russia's ability to earn money from exports by sea.

Ukrainian sea and air drones have struck tankers, naval ships and port facilities here, and Russia has responded by restricting ship-tracking data. Ships trading with Russian ports face high war-risk premiums and sanctions risks.
