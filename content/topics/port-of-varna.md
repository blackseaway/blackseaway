Varna is Bulgaria's largest Black Sea port and the home of the Bulgarian Navy. It handles bulk, container and general cargo and is a centre of Bulgaria's maritime industry, from shipping companies to crewing agencies.

The war has come close to Varna. Drones have struck merchant ships off the Bulgarian coast, drifting mines and drones have been found nearby, and Bulgaria has held large naval and air exercises in response. The EU's new Maritime Security Hub will have an office in Varna.
