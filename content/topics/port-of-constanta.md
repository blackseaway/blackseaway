Constanța is Romania's main seaport and the largest port on the Black Sea. It is linked to the Danube by the Danube–Black Sea Canal and to Central Europe by rail and road, which makes it a natural hub for Ukrainian, Moldovan and Central European cargo.

Since 2022 the port has handled a large share of Ukrainian grain transit. It has also become a focus of Black Sea security: the EU's new Maritime Security Hub will have an office in Constanța, and Romania's offshore gas projects lie nearby.
