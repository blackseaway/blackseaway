Burgas, in the south of Bulgaria's coast, is the country's main port for oil, bulk and general cargo and serves the nearby refinery. It lies on the route between the Bosphorus and the ports of Romania and Ukraine, which puts it close to the busiest shipping lanes of the western Black Sea.
